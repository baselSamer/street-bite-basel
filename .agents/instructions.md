The project is to build and deliver StreetBite V1.0, a production-oriented Flutter application. Users should be able to discover street-food vendors, explore meals from a live API, place orders, save favourites, and manage their profiles. The application must support English and Arabic, light and dark themes, and a clean, testable architecture. Follow the design reference and complete the requirements, documentation, and beta delivery evidence specified below.
